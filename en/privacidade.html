Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Your self-care is personal, and so is your data. This policy explains in plain language what Cafuné keeps and why, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Brazil’s LGPD.

1. Controller

Cafuné (meucafune.app) is a project by Gonçalo de Freitas, sole trader (Portuguese tax number 221637303) based in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. Contact: meucafune@gmail.com. Data protection contact: meucafune@gmail.com.

2. What data we use

  • Without an account: everything stays on your device (name or nickname, steps, mood, Dudu’s personality, look and scenery). We only send anonymous usage statistics (see section 5).
  • With an account: your email and the same app data, stored in the cloud.
  • Friends: your friend code, friend list, cuddles, notes and stickers exchanged, the days you did steps (for the Slow buddies plant) and a short profile only your friends can see (name or nickname, Dudu’s personality and look, today’s steps).
  • Reminders: if you turn them on, your device’s technical notification address, time zone, language and chosen time.
  • Purchases and subscription: plan, expiry, packs bought and Stripe identifiers. Payment happens on Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Website waitlist: name, email and the Dudu you picked.

3. Your mood is sensitive data

Mood entries can say something about your emotional health. They only go to the cloud if you create an account, are protected so only you can access them, and are never sold, shared for advertising or used for ads. Friends cannot see your mood.

4. Why we use it

  • To make the app work and save your progress (performance of contract).
  • To send your sign-in code and, if you ask, reminders and cuddle alerts (consent).
  • To bill and manage subscriptions and purchases (contract and legal obligations).
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, with anonymous statistics (legitimate interest).
  • To tell you about the launch (waitlist, consent).

5. Usage statistics

To know whether Cafuné is helping, we record simple events like "opened the app", "did a step" or "tapped Cafuné+", linked to a random device identifier (and to your account, if you have one). We use no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking tools. We keep these records for up to 13 months.

6. Providers

Supabase (database in São Paulo, Brazil, and server functions), Netlify (website and waitlist), Stripe (payments) and Brevo (sign-in code emails). Some are outside the EU; in those cases they use Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. They only process data to provide these services.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, portability or deletion of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. In the app: "Start over" erases your progress; "Delete my account" (Dudu → Your account) deletes your account and all cloud data immediately; reminders can be turned off on their card. For anything else, email meucafune@gmail.com and we reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

8. How long

We keep data while your account exists. If you delete your account, the data is deleted. Payment records stay with Stripe for as long as the law requires.

9. Children

Cafuné is not made for children under 13.
