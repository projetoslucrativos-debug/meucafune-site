2. What Cafuné is

Cafuné is a self-care and habits app with a virtual pet, Dudu. It helps you build tiny daily steps of care, track your mood, do breathing exercises, get reminders and send cuddles to friends.

3. Cafuné is not treatment

Cafuné is not a health service, does not diagnose anything and does not replace medical or psychological care. If you are going through a hard time, please reach out to a professional. You can find a free helpline in your country at findahelpline.com. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

4. Your account

You can use Cafuné without an account, with data kept only on your device, or sign in with your email to keep Dudu in the cloud and use friends, reminders and purchases. You are responsible for access to your email. You must be at least 13; under-18s need permission from a parent or guardian. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Dudu → Your account → Delete my account.

5. Personalities and no-filter mode

Dudu can have different personalities, including Roaster Dudu, who makes jokes. The optional no-filter mode uses swear words and should only be turned on by users aged 16 or over (16+). It’s all humor, never a real insult. You can switch personality or turn the no-filter mode off at any time. If your mood today is "down", Dudu stops joking.

6. Friends

You can invite friends with a code. Friends only see your name or nickname, your Dudu’s personality and look, how many steps you did today and which days you both did steps (the Slow buddies plant). Friends can send you one cuddle a day, ready-made notes picked from a list (no free text) and duplicate stickers. There is no chat and no public profiles. Be kind: don’t use Cafuné to bother anyone.

Invite reward: when 3 of your friends have an account and have done at least one step, you get Baby Dudu and 1 free month of Cafuné+, once per account. If you already have an active recurring subscription, you get Baby Dudu only. The free month does not renew and nothing is charged at the end. Fake accounts or accounts created only to get the reward may lose it.

7. Cafuné+ (subscription)

Cafuné is free and has an optional subscription, Cafuné+, with extras (exclusive colors, seasonal packs included, every wardrobe treat and choosing your reminder time). Prices are shown in the app before you pay.

  • Cancel: at any time in the app: Dudu → Cafuné+ → Manage or cancel subscription. Cafuné+ stays on until the end of the period already paid.
